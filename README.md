# DrzavnaMatura
# State Graduation Exam Management System

## About the Project

The State Graduation Exam (Državna matura) is an examination system in Serbia designed for students completing their secondary education. It evaluates the knowledge and skills students have acquired during their studies and serves as an important part of the transition from secondary education to higher education.

This project is designed to simplify and improve the organization of students taking the State Graduation Exam. Its main purpose is to provide a faster, more efficient, and more organized way of managing student information, exam assignments, and classroom distribution.

## Main Purpose

Organizing a large number of students for graduation exams can be a time-consuming and complicated administrative task. Students need to be registered, assigned to appropriate exam groups and classrooms, and organized according to the subjects and types of exams they will take.

This application aims to simplify that process by bringing the necessary information and organizational tasks together in one place. It is primarily intended for school staff and administrators responsible for registering students and preparing the exam schedule.

## Features

The system is intended to support:

- **Student Management** – Registering students and managing their information.
- **Classroom Assignment** – Organizing students into appropriate classrooms.
- **Exam Organization** – Assigning students to the exams they need to take.
- **Multiple Exam Types** – Supporting different types of graduation exams, including the Professional Matura (Stručna matura).
- **Student Distribution** – Helping administrators organize students into groups based on their exam requirements.
- **Efficient Organization** – Making the entire process faster, clearer, and easier to manage.

## Goals

The main goal of this project is to reduce the time and effort required to organize the State Graduation Exam. By simplifying repetitive administrative tasks and keeping student information organized, the system helps minimize errors, improve efficiency, and make the exam preparation process more manageable.

Ultimately, this project aims to provide a practical tool that makes graduation exam organization easier for both school administrators and the educational institutions they work for.
